Product Design Thinking

Design Philosophy

Problem

Meetings generate valuable insights but consistently fail at execution. The disconnect between discussion and action leads to lost opportunities and stalled projects.

Insight

The gap is not summarization, but a lack of structured follow-through. Most tools stop at notes — we need to bridge the chasm between conversation and completion.

Solution

Designed an agent-based workflow connecting meeting → execution. From voice to action items, from decisions to follow-ups — creating a complete闭环 that ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

Key Tradeoffs

⚖️ Automation vs Control

Balanced automation with user control by introducing editable action items, allowing refinement without losing efficiency.

⚡ Latency vs Completeness

Optimized for speed by limiting summary depth in MVP — fast insights over exhaustive detail.

🎯 Precision vs Recall

Prioritized action item precision over recall to build user trust — better to miss one than create noise.

Future Vision

Extend to cross-tool agent orchestration — email, calendar, docs

Enable proactive AI follow-ups based on task status

Integrate with enterprise workflows — Jira, Teams, Notion

"From conversation to completion — closing the execution gap, one meeting at a time."
